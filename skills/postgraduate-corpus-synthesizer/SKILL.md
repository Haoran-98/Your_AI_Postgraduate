# Postgraduate Corpus Synthesizer

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/artifact-contracts.md`, `evidence-policy.md`, and `deep-fulltext-causal-research-subskill.md`.

## Workflow

1. Require per-paper completion or explicit blocked status.
2. Group validated memories by question, method, variable, dataset, experiment, finding, limitation, mechanism, and causal status.
3. Identify convergence, contradiction, boundary conditions, and missing evidence.
4. Update variables, mechanisms, datasets/artifacts, supported claims, gaps, causal assumptions, hypotheses/tests, sources, and survey pages.
5. Generate idea-specific support and refutation from the corpus rather than copying a generic conclusion into every paper.
6. Update index, hot, and log.

Do not count metadata-only papers as evidence coverage.
