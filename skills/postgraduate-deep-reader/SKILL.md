# Postgraduate Deep Reader

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/deep-fulltext-causal-research-subskill.md` and `evidence-policy.md` before reading.

## Per-Paper Extraction

Extract only from readable full text:

- research question and contribution;
- method, variables, constructs, dataset, artifacts, and experiment design;
- primary findings with important numbers;
- limitations, threats, boundary conditions, and causal interpretation;
- transferable mechanism;
- support and counterevidence for the current idea;
- contrarian hypothesis and a verification or falsification experiment.

Distinguish the paper's own contribution from cited background. Preserve exact locations and do not promote external links to verified evidence until read.

Update the paper card and corpus-level evidence pages after the paper is complete.
