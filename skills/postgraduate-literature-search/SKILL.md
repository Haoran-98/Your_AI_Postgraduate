# Postgraduate Literature Search

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/autoresearch-protocol.md`, `deep-fulltext-causal-research-subskill.md`, and `privacy-publication.md`.

## Source Order

Prefer official conference, journal, ACL Anthology, arXiv, OpenReview, DOI, publisher, dataset, code, and project pages. Search relevant venues named by the research domain; the existing workflow explicitly includes ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLING, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, IJCAI, AIED, EDM, LAK, CHI, CSCW, UIST, SIGCSE, and Learning@Scale.

## Output

For every important source record venue/year, official URL, why it matters, triggering idea or paper, evidence level, and whether it supports, refutes, or complicates the direction.

External linkages remain hypotheses and search routes until full text is read.
