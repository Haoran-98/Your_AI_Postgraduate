# Obsidian Writing

Use concise durable notes with YAML frontmatter:

```markdown
---
type: paper | concept | variable | mechanism | intervention | dataset | idea | bridge
domain: TBD
status: seed
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
---

# Title

Summary.

## Links

- [[Related Note]]
```

Prefer stable titles, Obsidian wikilinks, and short pages that can be extended later.
