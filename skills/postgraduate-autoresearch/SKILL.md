# Postgraduate AutoResearch

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/autoresearch-protocol.md`, `governance.md`, and `evidence-policy.md`.

## Workflow

1. Convert the idea into public-safe search keywords.
2. Search current high-quality foundations, recent work, datasets, methods, and gaps.
3. Build a literature map with inclusion and exclusion reasons.
4. Generate multiple related idea variants with feasibility and evidence needs.
5. Mark novelty `searched`, `partially searched`, or `unsearched`.
6. Save durable survey, gap, source, and idea notes when persistence is requested.

Do not call a direction novel merely because it was not found in a shallow search.
