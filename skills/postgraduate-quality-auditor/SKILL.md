# Postgraduate Quality Auditor

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/evidence-policy.md`, `artifact-contracts.md`, and `privacy-publication.md`.

## Gates

- Every paper is fulltext-read or concretely blocked.
- Blocked papers do not support verified claims.
- Paper-card and BibTeX counts match.
- Authors, affiliations when available, URL/DOI, citation key, and BibTeX survive derived layers.
- Validated memories have source chunk pointers and accepted evidence match types.
- Core knowledge kinds are present when supported by the paper.
- Abnormally low memory counts are investigated against rejected candidates and PDF layout.
- RAG retrieves memories, original chunks, and bibliography.
- No unfinished process remains.
- Tests, diff checks, path privacy, and secret scans pass.

Report residual risk explicitly; program completion alone is not research completion.
