# Postgraduate Domain Router

Read `../postgraduate-common/references/governance.md`, `routing-policy.md`, and `idea-batch-ingestion.md`.

## Workflow

1. Reduce the idea to its core object, population, mechanism, and research objective.
2. Prefer Education, Military, Information_Opinion, or Mental_Health when one clearly owns the idea.
3. Create a new English domain slug for cross-domain infrastructure or another unmatched topic.
4. Output `Postgraduate_<EnglishDomainSlug>` and the rationale.
5. Never merge unrelated ideas into a broad global vault.

Chinese text may remain in note content, but generated paths use English ASCII slugs.
